Nelea Motriuc (FAO)      10:02
Dear Sophany, thank you for raising this important question. FAO has developed a global initiative called RENOFARM - Reduce the Need for Antimicrobials on Farms for Sustainable Agrifood Systems Transformation. It is an action-oriented, country-focused, and country-led initiative responding to the global agrifood systems transformation agenda that engages the entire production chain, from farm to fork, and strengthens partnerships, including public-private. It is based on the Farm 5Gs framework that helps farmers to operate the necessary change to prevent the use of antimicrobials at sourse. The initiaitve has a community of practice available to veterinarians, farmers and all other stakeholders willing to learn form each other and collaborate. Here is more info: https://www.fao.org/antimicrobial-resistance/background/fao-role/renofarm/en/
