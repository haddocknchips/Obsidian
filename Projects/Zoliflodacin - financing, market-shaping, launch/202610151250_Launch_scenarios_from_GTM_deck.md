## Low, base and high scenarios

Three internally consistent versions of how the Thailand launch could play out, built from the same set of assumptions set at pessimistic, central and optimistic values, so that the economics can be shown as a range rather than a single forecast.

Each scenario fixes a value for the main drivers together, so they hang together as a story rather than mixing a low volume with a high price:

|Driver|Low|Base|High|
|---|---|---|---|
|Positioning|Reserve/treatment-failure only|Second-line with test-of-cure in guideline|Broader use, path to first-line|
|Patient volume|~800/yr, slow growth|Supplier trajectory (~6,100 peak)|GARDP resistance scenario (10,000+)|
|Channel mix|Mostly private, public pilot only|80/20 public/private once NLEM lands|Public reimbursement earlier and broader|
|Public price|Syneos corridor (~$3)|NLEM corridor (~€3–4.5)|~$15 figure holds|
|Private price|~$32–38|Lower end of launch range|Launch range achievable|
|Transfer price|Stays near $43 longer|~$25 by 2030/31|Faster reduction|

The figures are the deck's own; the point is that it contains all three but never combines them into coherent cases.

**What each scenario produces:** revenue to the supplier, the size and timing of the affordability gap and the supplier-margin gap, and how long GARDP or a funder would need to carry them. Low tells you the exposure if things go badly; high tells you what the ask becomes at scale (the deck notes it roughly triples); base is what you'd build the investment case on.

**Why it matters for the Board:** it shows which assumptions actually move the answer. If the gap is similar across all three, financing design can proceed; if it swings widely, the Board sees that the priority is resolving positioning or pricing first, not choosing a mechanism.

The proposal's caveat "where the available evidence supports this" is deliberate: if the volume and price inputs are too thin by the Board date, the honest output is a base case with sensitivities on two or three drivers, rather than three full scenarios that would imply more confidence than exists.
